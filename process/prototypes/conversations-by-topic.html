      How the umbrellas were made

      Each call was recorded under one assigned topic. The 64 topics that appear here are grouped into ten umbrellas by subject. The grouping is an editorial choice, not part of the corpus, and it lives in one table at the top of the build script.

      How the shares are counted

      Each word takes the color of the utterance it belongs to. Shares for an umbrella or a topic pool all of its words, so longer calls weigh more than shorter ones.

      What is included

      The 650 conversations that have a syntactic parse, out of 1,155. Two topics have no parsed calls and do not appear. The topic is the prompt the callers were given, not a coding of what they said.
